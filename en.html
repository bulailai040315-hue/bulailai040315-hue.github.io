Research · Writing · Storytelling

I believe good research requires not only rigorous analysis but also clear expression. My interests span business, AI, finance, and culture — I hope to connect data, perspectives, and people through writing.

01 · About Me

I graduated with a bachelor's degree in Chinese Language and Literature (Teachers' Training), with a GPA of 4.21/5.0, and was a recipient of the National Scholarship.

During my undergraduate years, I interned at Datayes, CBN (China Business Network), Shanghai Media Group, and Changzhou Wujin Media Center, authoring over 560 financial news articles. My work spanned financial news production, research translation, data processing, TV news gathering and editing, and new media operations. These experiences shaped my approach to research, analysis, and expression — and made me realize that I prefer finding patterns in complex information and distilling them into clear, structured narratives, rather than simply recording data.

Next, I will pursue a Master's degree in Chinese Language and Literature at City University of Hong Kong, aiming to further strengthen my research skills and continue exploring the intersection of humanities research, business analysis, and AI tools.
